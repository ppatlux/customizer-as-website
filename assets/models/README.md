## 3D print .STL files

You will find all 3D printable files in this folder sorted by type of modularity, with some prebuilds:

* bottoms: Base part to click middle or top parts as well attach bumpers.
	* bumpers: Attach to starter bottom, like accessories or other devices.
	* motion: Tires, pulleys and gears in multiple sizes.

* middle: the band in between bottoms and tops, that initially was design for the main circuit board and battery but it has more potential and it can be stack with another or spacers.
	* faces: Slides into middle; decorate and swap eyes, fit multiple displays and even sensors.

* tops: Click on middle or bottoms, head part commonly use for LED ring or sensors.
	* hats: Decorative parts to put on top of tops.

* football: Soccer puzzle field.
* misc: Miscellaneous common and useful parts or tools.

kits
* walk & roll: AKA Ninja, arms and humanoid parts.
* bricks: Lego and Technic compatible parts, mainly for invent.

There are also some neutral .STEP files that work as templates for importing into any CAD software for further modification in sizes or details. For quick changes use Makers Empire https://www.makersempire.com/download/
